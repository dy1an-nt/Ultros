Questions, answered.

What counts as a scored row?

One dataset item, run through one prompt version on one model, and scored by a judge. A run of 1,000 items across 4 models is 4,000 scored rows.

Do I bring my own model keys?

Yes. Ultros calls providers with your keys, so you pay providers directly at your negotiated rates. We never mark up tokens.

What happens when I hit my row limit?

Runs queue instead of failing. You can raise the limit from settings; overage is billed at $2 per 1,000 rows, capped at the price of the next tier.
