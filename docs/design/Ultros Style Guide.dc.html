Ultros

Jade on near-black. The brand color is the pass color.

Ultros runs evaluations — its entire promise is the moment a prompt version passes. So the signature color is the color of a passing eval: a calm, desaturated jade. Competitors own bright green-on-white, blue, and yellow; nobody in this category owns muted jade on a deep green-black canvas.
