Runs are first-class

Every execution is versioned, tracked, and comparable — prompt, model, parameters, and dataset pinned together. Nothing is a one-off.

Judges you can trust

AI-as-judge with rubrics you define, calibrated against your own human labels — so a score of 8.7 means the same thing next month.

Regressions, caught

Four lines to your first eval.

Point Ultros at a prompt and a dataset. It fans out across models, scores every output, and reports the delta against your last version.
